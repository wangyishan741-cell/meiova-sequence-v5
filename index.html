IVM is a type of IVF. Eggs are collected earlier and finish maturing in the laboratory, rather than inside the body. For selected patients, this can mean fewer hormone injections and monitoring visits.

What changes in your care?

A side-by-side guide to discuss with your physician.

Part of your careConventional IVFIVM-based IVF
Where eggs matureIn the body, before collection.In the laboratory, after collection.
MedicationOvarian stimulation is used to support maturation before retrieval.Selected patients may need fewer hormone injections. The protocol determines medication.
MonitoringMonitoring tracks the response to stimulation before retrieval.Selected patients may need fewer monitoring visits. Tests are still arranged as needed.
Visits and locationsThe clinic and individual protocol determine visits and locations.Here, consultations can begin from home, tests near home and retrieval in New York. Some people need a second trip.

Fewer injections does not mean no medication. This comparison does not establish equal outcomes, greater safety or a shorter treatment duration. Your physician explains the evidence and alternatives for you.

This diagram compares where maturation happens, not success rates or safety. Medication and monitoring depend on the physician's plan.

For the IVF pathway described here, eggs mature, are fertilized and grow in the lab. Embryos are then frozen, with transfer arranged for a later cycle. Egg freezing preserves unfertilized eggs instead; ask your physician about the appropriate pathway.

Every medical decision, procedure and lab step is made by the licensed physicians and embryologists of New Hope Fertility Center in New York. With IVM, instead of weeks of hormones to ripen eggs inside your body, eggs are collected earlier and matured in their lab.

Your questions,
answered clearly.

Is IVM really IVF?

Yes. IVM is a type of IVF. The big difference is that your eggs finish growing in the lab instead of in your body, which usually means far fewer shots and far fewer visits.

Who provides my medical care?

All medical and laboratory care is provided by New Hope Fertility Center in New York and its physicians and embryologists. Meiova is the technology and coordination network around that care: your questions, questionnaire, records, scheduling, reminders and video visits. Meiova does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.

Will I need any shots or medication?

For selected patients, far fewer than traditional IVF. The physician will explain exactly what your plan includes before you start.

How many times do I have to travel?

Plan on one trip to New York for the egg retrieval. Some people need a second one. Everything else happens from home, and your transfer can be in New York or at a partner clinic closer to you.

Why are the embryos frozen?

Your eggs need a few days in the lab to finish growing and become embryos, so every embryo is frozen and the transfer happens later. It also gives your body time to rest.

Do I need tests before I start?

No. You can start the questionnaire today. If you need a blood test or ultrasound, we help you arrange it near home.

How much does it cost?

Before you commit to anything, you get the full price in writing, with exactly what it includes and anything that is separate. No surprises.

Can I do this if I don't live in New York?

We are starting with a small first group and adding more states step by step. Tell us where you live, and if care is not available in your state yet, we will save your spot and let you know as soon as it is.

Could IVM
be right for you?

IVM is most often considered for people who:

  • Are between about 18 and 35
  • Have PCOS, or have been told they have plenty of eggs
  • Want fewer shots and fewer appointments
  • Have been worried about their ovaries overreacting to fertility drugs
  • Live far from a fertility specialist
  • Can make a trip to New York for the retrieval

Our promise to be honest with you.

IVM is not right for everyone, and conventional IVF may be the better option. A licensed physician assesses which approach fits your circumstances. Pregnancy and live birth are not guaranteed.
